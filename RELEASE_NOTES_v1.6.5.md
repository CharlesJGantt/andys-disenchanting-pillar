# Andy's Disenchanting Pillar v1.6.5

The first public release of Andy's Disenchanting Pillar.

## Highlights

- Extract one selected enchantment into a reusable enchanted book.
- Preserve the source item and every unselected enchantment.
- Use compact pillars, two-block stacked pillars, or lectern-style altars.
- Craft a compact pillar from amethyst, lapis, copper, an enchanting table, obsidian, and two matching body blocks; assemble a tall pillar from two matching pillars; or convert one pillar into an altar with two copper ingots and one amethyst shard.
- Craft 48 variants using vanilla body materials.
- Recolor emissive runes and floating glyphs with all 16 normal dyes.
- Pay configurable XP, ordinary-book, and amethyst-shard costs.
- Remove Mending and curses using configurable special surcharges.
- Reapply extracted enchantments without runaway hidden prior-work penalties.
- Use the included signed CWES guide and operator settings menu.
- Play with Vibrant Visuals or Classic rendering without experimental toggles.
- Keep extraction meaningful in Survival through deliberately substantial mid-game crafting and recurring book, shard, and XP costs.

## Download

Install the combined file:

`Andys Disenchanting Pillar v1.6.5.mcaddon`

Both the behavior pack and resource pack are included and must remain active at matching versions.

## Requirements

- Minecraft Bedrock 1.26.0 or newer
- No experimental gameplay toggles

Back up important worlds before installing or updating any add-on.
